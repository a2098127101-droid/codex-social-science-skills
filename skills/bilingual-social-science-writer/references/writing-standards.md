# Writing Standards

## Chinese manuscript

- Avoid policy-slogan prose unless quoting policy material as evidence.
- Replace generic phrases such as "具有重要意义" with specific research contributions.
- State the research question early.
- Organize the literature review by debates and mechanisms, not by "domestic vs foreign" unless required.
- Keep conclusion claims within evidence boundaries.

## English manuscript

- Do not translate Chinese sentences mechanically.
- Prefer direct verbs and specific claims.
- Avoid inflated novelty claims.
- Make contribution legible to the target field.
- Use causal terms only when design supports causal inference.

## Abstract requirements

Include:

- research problem
- object/sample/materials
- method
- main evidence-bound finding
- theoretical contribution
- practical implication
- limitation or boundary when needed

## Literature review requirements

Distinguish:

- empirical gap
- theoretical gap
- methodological gap
- contextual gap
- temporal gap
- data gap
- normative/policy gap

Do not cite papers that have not been checked.
